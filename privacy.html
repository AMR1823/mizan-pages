Mizan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

English

Mizan is a personal budgeting app used only by its owner. With your explicit consent, it reads your bank account balances and transactions through Enable Banking (PSD2 account information service) and reads payment notifications on your device.

  • All data is stored only on your device, encrypted.
  • An optional backup can be saved to your own Google Drive.
  • No data is sold, shared or sent to any other third party.
  • The app never sees or stores your bank password.
  • You can revoke bank access at any time from the app or from your bank, and delete all data by uninstalling the app.

Italiano

Mizan è un'app personale di gestione del budget, usata solo dal suo proprietario. Con il tuo consenso esplicito legge saldi e movimenti del conto tramite Enable Banking (servizio di informazione sui conti PSD2) e le notifiche di pagamento sul dispositivo.

  • Tutti i dati restano solo sul tuo dispositivo, cifrati.
  • Un backup facoltativo può essere salvato sul tuo Google Drive.
  • Nessun dato viene venduto, condiviso o inviato a terzi.
  • L'app non vede né salva mai la password della banca.
  • Puoi revocare l'accesso in qualsiasi momento dall'app o dalla banca, ed eliminare tutti i dati disinstallando l'app.

العربية

  • كل البيانات محفوظة على جهازك فقط ومشفرة.
  • يمكن حفظ نسخة احتياطية اختيارية على جوجل درايف الخاص بك.
  • لا يتم بيع أو مشاركة أو إرسال أي بيانات لأي طرف آخر.
  • البرنامج لا يرى ولا يحفظ كلمة سر البنك أبدًا.
  • يمكنك إلغاء الوصول للبنك في أي وقت، وحذف كل البيانات بحذف البرنامج.
